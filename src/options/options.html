YouTube (companion app)

YouTube downloads run through a small helper app on your computer that uses yt-dlp. Files are saved by the helper to your Downloads folder (plus the subfolder above), not through Chrome. Only download videos you have the right to keep.

Setup: install yt-dlp (and ffmpeg for 1080p and above), then from the companion download run bash companion/install.sh EXTENSION_ID on macOS/Linux, or on Windows powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File companion\install.ps1 EXTENSION_ID.
